## Prerequisites

Basic Adobe software skills:

1. For example, understand concepts such as layers, selections, CMYK, and RGB;
2. Know how to scale and move images;

Enough patience

## Research

First, investigate the business card format: the standard finished business card size in Taiwan and Chinese-speaking regions is 90 × 54 mm

Discuss the business card style you want with ChatGPT and ask it to return some reference images

<img src="/images/ai-business-card/obsidian-card-mockup.png" alt="" width="546">

Next, import the logo assets and continue discussing the style, asking ChatGPT to keep generating images until you are satisfied.

Finally, ask GPT to provide all the typeface information in the generated image as a reference for the design file

<img src="/images/ai-business-card/obsidian-design-guide.png" alt="">

## Execution

Next, create a new canvas in Adobe Illustrator and add a 3 mm bleed

<img src="/images/ai-business-card/obsidian-illustrator-new-document.png" alt="" width="605">

The remaining work is simple tasks such as adding type, moving elements, and aligning them. For small operational questions, ask AI for answers.

<img src="/images/ai-business-card/obsidian-process.jpg" alt="">

## Printing

After the design is complete, export it as an Adobe PDF and send it to a print shop. The finished product is shown below.
