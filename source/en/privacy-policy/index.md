# Wujian Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Wujian (the “App”) is a mobile application for photographing, recognizing, and organizing personal items. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Information stored locally

Item records, categories, original images, exported files, and app settings are primarily stored on your device. The App does not require a separate App account and does not automatically upload content simply because you create an inventory locally.

## 2. Camera permission

The App uses camera permission to photograph items. The camera is accessed only when you use the capture feature. Denying camera permission does not affect features that do not require the camera.

## 3. AI recognition and third-party services

When you actively run AI recognition, the App sends the relevant images and recognition request content to the vision-model service selected or configured in the App settings. You choose the service endpoint, model, and API Key; the App stores the API Key in the device’s secure storage.

Model providers may independently retain or process the content they receive, and their services may incur charges. Their privacy policies and terms of service govern processing, retention, cross-border transfers, and training use. The App does not make retention, training-use, or compliance promises on behalf of any third-party provider.

## 4. Sharing, advertising, and analytics

The App does not sell your item records or images. The current version does not integrate advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. When you use the system sharing feature to export or share a file, the file is handled by the system or third-party app you choose.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete or clear it in the App, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App may remove local device data, so back up important content before uninstalling, upgrading, or reinstalling. Deletion and retention of content already sent to a third-party model provider are governed by that provider’s policy.

## 6. Security

The App uses secure storage for API Keys where available and requires HTTPS for remote model endpoints. Configure only trusted service endpoints and avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information in item images or requests.

## 7. Policy updates and contact

This policy may change when the App’s features or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a new effective date. To contact the developer, please use [GitHub](https://github.com/GreatAndyC).
